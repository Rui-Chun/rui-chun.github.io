About Me

I am a senior researcher at Microsoft Research Asia - Vancouver, working with Dr. Lili Qiu.

I completed my PhD study at Yale University, advised by Wenjun Hu and Lin Zhong. I also worked closely with Lili Qiu at MSRA Shanghai. During my PhD, I worked on designing and building metasurface based wireless networking systems.

Research Interests

  • Wireless networking and sensing
  • Metasurface-based wireless systems
  • Sensing for embodied AI
